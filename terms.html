1. Using NOIR.

These terms apply when you visit NOIR., create an account or place an order. By using the store you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use it. Please also read our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your information.

2. Your account

You need an account to place an order. Give us correct details, keep your password private and tell us if you think someone else has used your account. You are responsible for what happens under your account. You must be 18 or over, or use the store with a parent or guardian.

3. Products and prices

We try to show our products, sizes and prices accurately, but colours can look different on different screens and small mistakes can happen. All prices are in Indian rupees (₹). The price you pay is the price shown at checkout when you place your order. We may change prices at any time, but that does not affect orders already placed.

If a product is listed at the wrong price or is out of stock, we may cancel the order and refund anything you have paid.

4. Orders

When you place an order, you are asking to buy the items. We confirm your order once we have accepted it. We may refuse or cancel an order, for example because of stock, a pricing error, an address we cannot deliver to, or suspected fraud. If we cancel an order you have paid for, we will refund you.

5. Payment

You can pay using the methods offered at checkout. If we need anything further from you to complete your payment, we will contact you using the details on your order. You must be authorised to use the payment method you choose.

6. Shipping and delivery

Delivery options, fees and estimated times are shown at checkout, and free shipping applies above the order value shown on the product and checkout pages. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees, and can be affected by things outside our control. Please check that your address and phone number are correct, because we cannot deliver to an incomplete address.

7. Exchanges and returns

We offer exchange support within 7 days of delivery. To be eligible, the item must be unused, unwashed and in its original condition with tags and packaging. To ask for an exchange, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number.

If an item arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, tell us as soon as you can and we will put it right, by replacement or refund. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under Indian consumer law.

8. Cancelling an order

To cancel, contact us as early as you can. We can usually cancel an order that has not been shipped. Once an order has shipped, the exchange terms above apply.
