1. Who we are

NOIR. ("NOIR.", "we", "us") is an online clothing store. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you browse, create an account or place an order, why we collect it, and the choices you have. You can reach us at support@noir.com or through our Contact page.

2. What we collect

WhenWhat we collect
You create an accountYour name, email address and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and stored in a protected (hashed) form. We never see it.
You place an orderYour name, email, phone number, delivery address, the items and sizes you ordered, the delivery and payment method you chose, and the order total.
You save an addressThe address details you enter, linked to your account so only you can see them.
You join our email listYour email address.
You contact usWhat you send us. If you use the WhatsApp form, your message goes through WhatsApp and is subject to their terms.
You use the siteYour bag, wishlist and checkout progress are kept in your own browser. We also handle technical information such as your IP address to keep the site secure and stop abuse.

3. How we use it

We use your information to:

  • create and run your account and let you sign in;
  • process, deliver and support your orders, including sending order updates and answering your questions;
  • send new arrivals and offers if you have joined our email list (you can stop these at any time);
  • keep the store secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix problems;
  • meet our legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser's storage (local storage and session storage) to remember your bag, your wishlist, your sign-in and your checkout progress. These are needed for the store to work. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads. You can clear stored data at any time from your browser settings, and signing out ends your session.

5. Who we share it with

We share only what is needed, with people who help us run the store:

  • Hosting and database. Supabase stores our database and handles account sign-in.
  • Delivery partners. Your name, phone number and address, so they can deliver your order.
  • Payment providers. When you pay online, the provider you choose processes your payment. We do not store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Google. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us.
  • Authorities. When the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. We keep order records for as long as we need them for delivery, exchanges, accounting and legal requirements. Email list sign-ups are kept until you ask us to remove them. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong or out of date (you can also edit your name and addresses in your account);
  • delete your account and personal information, subject to records we must keep by law;
  • stop sending you emails, or withdraw a consent you gave earlier.

Write to support@noir.com and we will reply as soon as we reasonably can. You also have the rights given to you by the laws of India that apply to personal data, including the right to complain about how your data is handled.

8. Keeping your information safe

We use secure connections, access rules that keep each customer's account data private, and a specialist provider for sign-in. No online service can be completely risk-free, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

9. Children

NOIR. is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please use the store with a parent or guardian. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our store or the law changes. The date at the top shows when it was last changed, and we will make important changes easy to see.

11. Contact us

Questions or complaints about your privacy? Email support@noir.com or use the Contact page.
